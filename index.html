FlowState — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026

FlowState is a productivity app (tasks, kanban, calendar, habits, focus timer).

Data we collect

None. FlowState has no accounts, no servers and no analytics or advertising SDKs. The app does not request internet access.

Data stored on your device

Your tasks, habits, notes, categories and settings are stored locally on your device only. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Backups and export

If you choose Export in Settings, the app creates a file and opens Android's share sheet. Where it goes (Drive, email, etc.) is your choice and governed by that service's policy.

Children

FlowState is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
